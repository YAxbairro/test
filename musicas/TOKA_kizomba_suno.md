# TOKA — kizomba sensual (letra da IA)

## Style of Music (926 caracteres)

```
Sensual Cape Verdean kizomba, 90 BPM, A minor, warm, intimate, smooth, late-night. Soft kizomba drum groove with deep kick, rimshot, shaker, round warm bass, nylon acoustic guitar, soft piano, gentle Rhodes, breathy flute. Balanced mix: every instrument present but restrained, nothing forced, smooth transitions, no abrupt changes. [Intro] piano and guitar, phone-like soft bell. [Verse] groove enters low, bass and rimshot. [Hook] full groove, guitar melody answers voice. [Middle] sound drop to piano and voice, halftime feel, then soft flute solo, groove returns gently. [Final Hook] modulates up one semitone, warmer. [Outro] guitar and piano fade. Male singer from Praia, Santiago, Cape Verde, singing in Cape Verdean Creole (Kriolu Badiu), NOT Portuguese, NOT Brazilian. Relaxed, soulful, breathy tenor, laid-back sensual delivery, clear diction, close-mic, upfront. No flashy synth or guitar solos. Clean mastered mix.
```
