# SEDU — final v4 (sem gospel)

## Style (Beat 05 v3)

```
Classic Angolan kizomba, urban kiz, slow sensual, 82 BPM, A minor, intimate, late-night, confident. Male R&B vocalist from Praia, Cape Verde, singing Cape Verdean Creole (Kriolu Badiu), smooth breathy tenor, single lead voice, relaxed phrasing slightly behind the beat, close-mic, upfront. Kizomba drum machine groove in 4/4: deep round kick on 1, the and of 2, and 3; soft finger-snap snare on 2 and 4; rimshot ghost note on the and of 4; shaker in soft swung 16ths. Warm sub bass locked to the kick, long notes, one gentle slide every 4 bars. Soft electric piano far in the background, short muted minor chords, one per bar. Nylon-string guitar, soft fingerpicked arpeggios answering each vocal line. Soft kizomba synth pluck on the hooks. Sparse, wide, spacious mix; energy grows gently each section. Warm analog tape feel, crisp clear vocals, polished loud master.
```

## Exclude

```
gospel, choir, organ, worship, church, backing vocals, ballad, EDM, synth lead, guitar solo, trap hi-hats, dance pop, female vocals
```

[Intro: felt piano chords and nylon guitar, filtered]
[Instrumental]

[Verse 1: kick and sub bass enter softly, guitar answers each line]
Ami djan staba konvensidu
ma Nka ta amaba otu alguén tão sedu.
Ma distinu muda tudu sentidu,
trazem bo, i mi N supera nhas medu.

Bu kompleta un vaziu
dentu di mi, ki ningen ka odjaba.
Di tantu friu ki dja N xinti,
bu foi kalor ki N ka sabia ma faltaba.

Bu fazen torna vivi,
ben na mi, sen bu pidim liçensa.
Undi amor dja moraba antigamenti,
bu fazi habita na auzensia.

Ami Nta amaba
un alguén ki oji é só segredu.
Mi N pui mal ku Kupidu,
ma talvez era mi
ki staba ku txeu medu.

Mas dja dura ki min
djan fazi paz ku kel pasadu.
Ma paz i stranhu:
N pensa ma fitcha um porta, bu entra pa otu ladu.

[Pre-Chorus: drums thin out, voice closer]
Na fundu era papu

ma korason nunka tinha dizistidu.

Na verdadi, ami N kria
un novu amor, ntinha medu admiti.
Chinti sabi,
i gossi pa kel k for,
bo Kim ta po di freti.

I bo, bu tchiga do nada
ku kel bistidu.
Bu konxi tudu nha pontu fraku,
mi kurvas ki ta difinubu.

[Verse 2: groove lighter, piano steps forward]

N só kre vive kel momentu.

Bu ten jeitu di pergunta

Mi ki djan fitcha sintimentu
pa ningen entra sen bate,
bu ka bate, bu simplismenti entra.
