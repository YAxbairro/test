Começar aqui

Cada comando tem um código (P1, R5, M3, C2). Abre, vê o que anexar, carrega em Copiar e cola no ChatGPT ou no Gemini. Depois marca como Feito, e o progresso fica guardado neste telemóvel.

  1. Produto (P): a garrafa com o logo. É a base de tudo o que vem a seguir.
  2. Roupa (R): mockups de cada peça sem modelo, para loja e catálogo.
  3. Modelos (M): fotos com os atletas a usar as peças.
  4. Campanha (C): imagens finais da campanha "Tira o peso da cabeça", com a frase para pôr por cima.
Anexa sempre o logoFicheiro logo-final.jpg. Sem ele, o modelo inventa outro.
Preto e brancoRoupa preta com logo branco, ou roupa branca com logo preto. Nada mais.
Logo pequenoPeito esquerdo, nuca ou anca. A marca grande fica só nas costas e nos hoodies.
Mesmo chatFaz cada grupo (P, R, M, C) no mesmo chat, para manter caras e peças iguais.
Texto em pósAs frases da campanha põe-nas no Canva ou no CapCut. A IA erra letras.
Guarda com o códigoGrava como R5_v2.png. Assim nunca te perdes.

R1BT-shirt oversized: pintura nas costasDiscos nos ombros, gorila no meio, texto em baixo · 9:16
1. Anexar:logo-final.jpg (gorila com atleta no supino)foto de referência (homem de costas no ginásio), opcional
2. Copiar e colar este comando:
Create a photorealistic product mockup of an OVERSIZED BLACK T-SHIRT seen from the BACK, worn by a male model.

ATTACHED IMAGES
- Image 1 = the brand logo (gorilla face with a barbell). Follow its design exactly: the barbell runs straight across the gorilla's forehead, the weight plates ARE the gorilla's ears, and a small person lying on a bench pressing the bar sits inside the forehead. Do not invent a different gorilla.
- Image 2 (if attached) = scene reference. Keep the same shot: man standing with his back to the camera, concrete gym wall, black pull-up rig, soft studio light. Only the shirt print changes.

WHAT THE BACK PRINT MUST LOOK LIKE
It is NOT a simple flat logo print. It is a large ARTWORK, like a black-and-white painting made directly on the fabric:
- hand-painted with visible brush strokes, dry-brush texture, ink splashes and soft grey airbrush shading
- the gorilla is painted with depth and volume (light hitting the brow, nose and cheeks, dark shadows around the eyes), but it keeps the exact shapes of the logo
- colors: only white, light grey and dark grey ink on the black fabric
- the ink looks soaked into the cotton, slightly worn, like a premium vintage streetwear piece

LAYOUT OF THE ARTWORK ON THE BACK (from top to bottom)
1. SHOULDERS: the two weight plates (the gorilla's ears) sit ON THE SHOULDERS, one on each side, right where the back meets the sleeves. They wrap slightly over the shoulder seams.
2. UPPER BACK: the barbell is one straight horizontal line across the upper back, from one shoulder plate to the other, just under the collar.
3. MIDDLE BACK: directly under the barbell, the gorilla face hangs down and fills the center of the back: forehead with the small bench-press figure, angry eyes, nose, closed mouth, chin. The chin ends around the lower ribs.
4. LOWER BACK: under the chin, the brand name in big bold condensed capital letters on two lines:
   TUTULEZA
   VICIADOS
5. Under the brand name, a smaller single line in clean capital letters:
   DEAR SELF, DON'T FOCUS ON YOUR NEGATIVITY.

The whole artwork is about 45 cm wide (it covers almost the full width of the back) and runs from the shoulders down to just above the hem.

TEXT RULES (very important)
- Write exactly: "TUTULEZA VICIADOS" and "DEAR SELF, DON'T FOCUS ON YOUR NEGATIVITY."
- No other words, no typos, no extra letters.

T-SHIRT
Oversized boxy fit, dropped shoulders, wide short sleeves, heavyweight 280 gsm black cotton, thick ribbed collar. Natural folds and fabric texture visible.

PHOTO STYLE
Photorealistic fashion e-commerce photo, 50mm lens, soft key light, sharp fabric and ink texture, vertical 9:16.

AVOID
Thin outline-only drawing, an empty gorilla face, a different gorilla, the plates floating away from the shoulders, colors, misspelled text, extra slogans.
