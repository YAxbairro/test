# Beat 01 — TEMPU (Suno)

Modo: **Custom** · Instrumental: **ON**

## Style of Music (470 caracteres)

```
Soulful boom bap rap instrumental, 91 BPM, A minor. Raw, intimate, human; warm and determined, not sad. Heavy warm bass, dusty punchy kick, dry snare, loose swung hats, soft clock-tick percussion motif. Dark felt piano, textured Rhodes, subtle cavaquinho and nylon guitar fragments with Cape Verdean morna color, restrained analog pads. Live feel, natural timing, strong dynamics, close mics, wide space for dense vocals. No EDM risers, no trap rolls. Instrumental only.
```

## Lyrics / estrutura (667 caracteres)

```
[Intro: lone felt piano phrase, clock tick, distant bass, tension]
[Verse: deep steady pocket, sparse chords, wide space for dense bars]
[Lift: bass walks more, Rhodes answers the vocal, drums gain weight]
[Drop: drums vanish, piano exposed, clock tick, one beat of silence]
[Verse: kick and bass slam back in, same pocket, fuller]
[Solo: short imperfect Rhodes phrase, human, never flashy]
[Shift: new kick pattern, halftime bass phrasing, same emotion]
[Break: piano and bass alone, cavaquinho whisper, most personal space]
[Final: drums heavier, harmony opens, bass grows, guitar fragments return]
[Outro: strip back to the opening piano phrase and the clock tick]
[End]
```

## Dicas
- Gera 4–6 versões e fica com a que tiver o melhor pocket no verso; o resto afina-se com Extend/Replace Section.
- O "clock-tick" é o motivo do TEMPU: se o Suno o exagerar, tira-o do Style e deixa-o só no Intro/Drop.
- Se o cavaquinho soar a pop, troca por "nylon guitar only".
